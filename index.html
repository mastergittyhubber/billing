Article A60298 Group 8 & Unlisted Exclusions

Master Section: Non-Covered Peripheral Nerves & Therapeutic Exclusions

Per LCD L40263 Item 7, any therapeutic block or denervation not explicitly listed as covered is non-covered. Note the distinction between clinical evidence (e.g., ASPN LEARN support) and payer non-coverage.

Thoracic: Intercostal & ESP

64420, 64421, 64620

LCD L40263 Verdict: Explicitly named non-covered in LCD & Group 8 for chronic pain management (includes Erector Spinae Plane blocks).

Exception: Acute pain (≤4 wks), rib trauma, and perioperative pain are outside the LCD scope. Chronic RFA is denied across commercial payers.

Tarsal Tunnel & Digital Nerves

64450, 28899, 64632*

LCD L40263 Verdict: Posterior tibial nerve blocks for chronic foot pain and digital nerve blocks for any chronic pain are explicitly named non-covered.

Morton's Denervation Trap: 64632* is in Group 8 non-covered (asterisk permits 1 diagnostic injection, NO thermal/chemical neurolysis).

Ganglion Impar (Walther)

64520, 64999, 64640

LCD L40263 Verdict: Explicitly named non-covered for both therapeutic blocks and denervation in chronic coccydynia/pelvic pain.

Payer Status: Medicare exclusion under L40263. Commercial plans (Anthem, Aetna, Cigna) consider Ganglion Impar blocks/RFA unproven.

Ilioinguinal / Iliohypogastric

64425, 64640

LCD L40263 Verdict: Unnamed in LCD, therefore excluded for therapeutic chronic pain management and denervation (64640).

Diagnostic Nuance: Because 64425 is not listed in Group 8, a 1-time diagnostic block is outside the LCD (not auto-denied). Repeat/therapeutic requires ABN.

Sciatic & Femoral Trunks

64445, 64447, 64640

LCD L40263 Verdict: Unnamed in LCD; excluded for chronic therapeutic injections and denervation (64640).

Diagnostic Nuance: Like 64425, codes 64445 and 64447 are not in Group 8, permitting a 1-time diagnostic block outside the LCD, or perioperative use.

Genitofemoral, LFCN & Cluneal

64450, 64640

LCD L40263 Verdict: Unnamed in LCD and have no dedicated CPT code—they default to 64450 / 64640, which are explicitly non-covered in Group 8.

64450 Trap: Even a 1-time diagnostic block of LFCN (meralgia paresthetica), genitofemoral, or cluneal nerves will deny under Group 8 because 64450 is blocked. ABN mandatory!

Hip Articular Branches (Fem/Obt)

64450, 64640

Clinical vs Payer: Clinically supported by ASPN LEARN and cooled-RFA studies, but omitted from LCD L40263 and billed via Group 8 non-covered codes (64450/64640).

Payer Status: Non-covered by Medicare (ABN + Modifier GA required) and deemed investigational by commercial plans (Anthem, Aetna, Cigna, UHC).

Shoulder Articular & Suprascapular RFA

64450, 64640

LCD L40263 Verdict: Suprascapular RFA and glenohumeral articular branch blocks/RFA (axillary, lateral pectoral) are investigational/non-covered.

Payer Status: Only suprascapular nerve block (64418) is covered for capsulitis/arthritis. Any shoulder RFA (64640) requires an ABN.

Pudendal Therapeutic & RFA

64430*, 64640

LCD L40263 Verdict: 64430* is in Group 8 with an asterisk: 1 diagnostic block is covered (Nantes criteria + KX), but therapeutic blocks and RFA are non-covered.

Audit Trap: Article A60298 publishes NO ICD-10 group for pudendal diagnostic blocks, creating an automated edit risk. Obtain a precautionary ABN for any repeat block.

Celiac, Splanchnic & Hypogastric

64530, 64680, 64520

LCD L40263 Verdict: Unnamed in LCD, so excluded for chronic non-malignant pain (e.g., chronic pancreatitis or non-cancer pelvic pain).

Malignancy Carve-Out: Pain related to malignancy refractory to medical management is explicitly outside the LCD and remains payable!

Sphenopalatine (SPG) & 64999

Policy Contradiction: 64505 and 64999 appear in Group 2 (CRPS), yet the LCD gives SPG no coverage criteria and 64999 is also on the Group 8 non-covered list!

Cryoablation, Biologics & Dry Needling

Fatal Claim Trap: Injecting a biologic or non-covered agent may cause Palmetto GBA to deny the ENTIRE claim under Benefit Policy Manual Ch. 16 §180.

Mandatory Modifier & Advance Beneficiary Notice (Form CMS-R-131) Protocol (Article A60298)

Modifier KX DIAGNOSTIC BLOCKS

Action: Append to the claim line for all initial covered diagnostic injections (Genicular, Occipital, Suprascapular, Trigeminal, Pudendal).

Meaning: Attests that requirements specified in LCD L40263 have been met. (Whether KX goes on the 2nd genicular block is unstated in A60298).

Modifier GA SIGNED ABN

Action: Append when a valid ABN (CMS-R-131) is signed (or patient refusal to sign is witnessed) for an expected medical necessity denial under L40263.

Billing Result: Denies as patient responsibility (PR-50), allowing patient balance billing. Never pair with GX.

Modifier GX VOLUNTARY ABN

Action: Append when a voluntary ABN was issued for a service excluded on grounds other than medical necessity (statutory exclusion).

Billing Result: Auto-denies per A60298. MUST pair with GY (GX-GY). Never pair with GA.

Modifier GY STATUTORY EXCLUSION

Action: Item or service is statutorily non-covered or does not meet the definition of any Medicare benefit. No ABN required.

Billing Result: Generates immediate PR denial for secondary commercial crossover. (Do not use GY for LCD medical necessity denials; use GA).

Modifier GZ PROVIDER LIABLE

Warning: Service expected to be denied as not reasonable and necessary under L40263, and NO ABN was signed prior to procedure.

Billing Result: Medicare denies as Contractual Obligation (CO-50). Practice absorbs 100% and cannot bill the patient!

Device & Implanted Hardware Rule: When a specific ablation device is used, list the specific device in the claim comments section (Box 19 / NTE). For patients with SCS, PNS, cardiac devices, or IT pumps, follow manufacturer instructions and document planning.

Procedural Documentation, Policy Contradictions & Audit Defense Checklist

Every element below must be recorded contemporaneously in the EMR to survive Palmetto GBA Targeted Probe and Educate (TPE) audits under L40263 and A60298.

1. Standardized Scales & Timed Readings

  • • Mandatory Scales (Genicular): Must document BOTH a pain scale (NRS, VAS, VRS, WOMAC, ICOAP) AND a disability/function scale (KOOS, Lequesne, dynamic weight-bearing) at baseline, and use the exact same pain scale after every diagnostic block.
  • • Relief % + Duration: Every diagnostic block requires pre- and post-procedure pain intensity recorded inside the anesthetic's expected window. Genicular, Suprascapular, and Trigeminal require ≥50% relief; Occipital requires >50% relief.
  • • Sympathetic Proof of Block: Stellate (64510) requires documenting a temporary Horner's syndrome; Lumbar (64520) requires documenting foot temperature rise ≥2°C (or ≥1.5°C warmer than contralateral foot at 20 min) plus baseline CRPS Severity Score (CSS).

2. Imaging & 7-Point RFA Note Checklist

  • × Bundled Guidance & Non-Covered Rule: 64454 & 64624 bundle imaging. Never bill 77002/77003-59 on somatic blocks when guidance is in the descriptor. Image guidance for a non-covered injection is also non-covered.
  • ✓ Ultrasound Rule: Store at least 1 retrievable image showing the needle at the target.
  • • Fluoroscopic RFA 7-Point Mandate: Note MUST record: (1) Nerves & anatomic location, (2) AP, oblique, and lateral views confirmed, (3) Contrast dye verification, (4) Sensory & motor testing response, (5) Total fluoro time & radiation dose, (6) Ablation temperature & duration, and (7) Patient tolerance/post-op status.

3. Strict 0.3–0.5 mL Volume & Mod 52

  • ! Genicular Volume Disqualifier: LCD L40263 mandates 0.3 to 0.5 mL per target nerve for genicular diagnostic blocks (64454). High injectate volume is an explicit disqualifier for RFA!
  • • Modifier 52 on 64454: 64454 requires injecting all 3 branches (superolateral, superomedial, inferomedial). If fewer than 3 branches are injected, you MUST append Modifier 52.
  • • Steroid & Biologics Rules: Carpal tunnel (20526) and Morton's (64455) require corticosteroid (LA alone is non-covered). Conversely, genicular diagnostic blocks are work-up only (no therapeutic block covered). Biologics (PRP, amniotic, dextrose) deny the entire claim!

4. Genicular KL Grade 3–4 & Disqualifiers

  • ! KL Grade 3 or 4 Required: Unlike older policies that accepted KL ≥2, LCD L40263 strictly requires radiographic Kellgren-Lawrence Grade 3 or 4 knee osteoarthritis.
  • × Post-TKA & 3-Month Washout Disqualifiers: Genicular block/RFA is non-covered after Total Knee Replacement (TKA) beyond the perioperative period. Also disqualified if the patient had an intra-articular knee injection or knee surgery within the last 3 months, or has inflammatory arthropathy.
  • • Informed Consent Specifics: Every procedure note must explicitly state that informed consent included the risks of nerve damage and worsening pain.

5. Sedation Ban & Same-Day Procedures

  • × Routine Sedation / MAC Non-Covered: While the LCD allows rare exceptions, Article A60298 states moderate/deep sedation, general anesthesia, and MAC "should not be billed except for during operative and perioperative nerve blocks." Plan on oral anxiolytics only; expect sedation lines to deny.
  • • Same-Day Injections & Electrical Stim: Electrical stimulation during a nerve block injection is non-covered. Performing a PNI on the same day as an ESI, facet, trigger point, or denervation is "not routinely necessary"—must document distinct medical necessity for each.
  • • Post-Op Anesthesia Block (64400–64530): Separately reportable only when requested by the operating surgeon after the PACU period ends, appending Modifier 59 or XU.

6. ASC Bilateral Rules & Policy Conflicts

  • • ASC vs. Professional Bilateral Billing: Professional claims report Modifier 50 (A60298 says "plus add-on code", though none exists). ASC Facility claims (Specialty 49) MUST NOT use Modifier 50—bill 2 separate lines with LT and RT (1 unit each).
  • ! RF Neurolysis Wording Conflict: Article A60298 states therapeutic RF neurolysis "may not be billed for clinical conditions other than trigeminal neuralgia" and caps it at 2/12m across all codes, yet LCD L40263 explicitly covers genicular RFA (2/knee/12m). Appeal any genicular denial with the LCD text.
  • ! 3-Month Genicular Relief Ambiguity: LCD asks for "≥50% improvement in pain, with consistent pain relief or function, for at least 3 months" before RFA—an impossibility for a short-acting 0.3–0.5mL diagnostic block. Document both immediate block duration/relief AND 3+ months of baseline pain/functional history.
